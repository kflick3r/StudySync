# StudySync
**AI Study Buddy: An Interactive Study Companion for Students**

StudySync is an AI-assisted study application designed to help students turn their own course materials into personalized and interactive study resources. The goal is to allow students to provide materials such as course notes and instructor-provided practice materials and use them to generate study guides, practice questions, and explanations grounded in those materials.

Rather than simply providing answers, StudySync is intended to act as a study buddy that helps students review concepts, practice what they have learned, and identify gaps in their understanding.

## Project Status

StudySync is currently in the planning and early development stage.

## Initial MVP

The first version of StudySync will focus on three core capabilities:

- Accept student-provided course materials as context.
- Generate study materials based on the provided content.
- Provide interactive study support and explanations based on the course materials.

Additional features will be considered after the core MVP is functional.
